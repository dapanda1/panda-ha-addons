# Utility CustConnect Water

Hourly water usage from CustConnect utility portals (`<utility>.custconnect.com/HomeConnect`, or a utility's own domain), imported into Home Assistant long-term statistics for the Energy dashboard, plus billing-period and status sensors and failure alerts.

See DOCS.md for configuration.

## Disclaimer

This is an unofficial, community add-on. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by CustConnect, its vendor, or any utility. It signs in to your utility's customer portal with the credentials you provide and reads the same usage data the portal shows you. You are responsible for complying with your utility's portal terms of use. The portal can change without notice, which may stop the add-on from working.
